- **Nothing Rune draws asks for a texture larger than the GPU makes, so an image or a layer larger than that draws smaller instead of ending the app:** the image atlas places a decoded image at its largest mip that fits the device's `max_texture_dimension_2d`, and a `Pixels::set` frame halved as its mips would be, in linear light, as it's copied in. It learns the limit before it places anything, from the smallest limit of any renderer made in the process until it is told its own, so a host that draws a large image once draws it on that frame; `ImageAtlas::set_max_side` tells it directly. `Renderer::upload_images` never makes a page texture the device would refuse (a validation error, which wgpu's default handler panics on). An effect layer wider or taller than the limit (a full-window blur's support, bucketed) keeps what the window shows of it and as much of its support around that as fits, and the glyph outline store grows no taller than the device's textures. WebGL2 and downlevel adapters report 2048 or 4096. The first time an image draws smaller it says so on stderr. A `Pixels::set` frame over 8192 pixels a side now draws, smaller, rather than not at all. The headless renderer refuses a canvas larger than the GPU's textures with a readable error.
