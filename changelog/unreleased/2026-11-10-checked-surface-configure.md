- **`embed::LayerHost` and the web canvas survive a configuration their surface refuses, and no drawable is sized past the device's largest texture:** both now configure through `window::configure`, as windows and overlays do, and it catches out-of-memory errors too. A LayerHost or canvas without a usable configuration draws nothing until it gets one: a refused configure and a drawable still lost or outdated after reconfiguring count toward one run of failures that only a drawn frame ends, retried on the next resize and by itself after 50 ms, doubling, five times, reported on stderr (the console on the web) when it begins and when it stops trying, and held while the host is suspended or zero-sized. On the web the configure's error scopes are read: WebGL2's at once through wgpu, WebGPU's through the browser's own scopes (wgpu's conversion panics on an internal error) when the browser answers, carrying the configure they answer so a late refusal never undoes a newer one, and waking the app to read it. A canvas that still fails after five tries says so on the console and, like LayerHost, waits for a resize; the app keeps running. `LayerHost::frame` returns `wake_after_secs` for its retry and stops reporting `animating` once it stops trying. Window and overlay drawables are held to 1 to the device's `max_texture_dimension_2d`: Windows has reported a client height wrapped to 65527 as a window appeared.
