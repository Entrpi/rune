- **Markdown caps nesting at 32, a table at 128 columns and a document at 100,000 blocks:** a quote or list opened deeper than 32 adds its blocks, text and all, to the 32nd, so 100,000 nested `>` or `-` from a broken or hostile model draw instead of overflowing the stack and aborting the app; a row's cells past the 128th are dropped, as GFM drops a row's cells past its header's; and once a document has built 100,000 blocks, list items and table rows, the rest of its source is one paragraph of its text as written, and an image that would stand as a block of its own stays in its line as its alt text. Whole documents and streams apply the caps alike. A deep list's copied indent stops at 255 columns instead of overflowing.
