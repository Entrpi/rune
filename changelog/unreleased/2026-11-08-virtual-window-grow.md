- **A virtual list in a window that grows mounts rows for its new height on the first pass, so the resize runs the view once.** A viewport laid out before keeps its height plus what the window grew since the last commit, up to its bound from the tree, instead of picking rows for the old height, finding the bottom blank and running the view again with every row mounted. A window that shrinks, or a viewport with no bound (inside a scroll, or in a window that fits its content), keeps its last height as before.
