- **Rune's Metal backend is the macOS default only from macOS 13**, which has the MSL 3.0 its shaders and precompiled libraries need; on macOS 11 and 12 an app draws with wgpu's backend instead of panicking at its first pipeline. `App::gpu_backend(GpuBackend::Metal)` or `RUNE_GPU_BACKEND=metal` there draws with wgpu too and says so once on stderr, and `describe_gpu` gives the reason.
